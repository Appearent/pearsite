1Assess

Tell us your goals and send what you have: existing translations (AI or human, in any language), a finished script, or one still in progress. We review it, then give you a clear roadmap for a painless release.

2Prepare

We get your script translation-ready, with the right format and context. Messy export or an odd file format? We sort it out, glossary included, and set you up so next time is easier.

3Translate

Our translators are human, native speakers who play games, and they tune every line to your script's voice. We work across many languages and keep adding more, from Chinese and Japanese to the world.

4Voice

On request, we voice your game professionally in Japanese or Chinese. Native voice talent is matched to each character's register, then recorded, directed and delivered to your spec.

5Ship

Native-speaker gamers play through your build, testing in the game itself right up to release. They catch text overflow, clipping, wrong variables and leftover source text. After launch, we watch your reviews and flag what needs a fix.
